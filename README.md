# Lydera

**An inclusive AI learning ecosystem that makes math accessible for visually impaired students.**

Lydera helps visually impaired students learn math independently, and helps their teachers prepare materials and understand each student's progress. Math notation, tables, and images are turned into text that screen readers can read aloud, and teachers review anything the AI produces before it reaches students.

Lydera is currently available for Android.

## Why Lydera

Math is one of the hardest subjects to access without sight. Formulas, symbols, and diagrams don't translate well into screen readers, and teachers often have to adapt every module and exercise by hand. Lydera was built from field research at SLBN A Citeureup to close that gap.

## Who it's for

- **Students**: visually impaired students who study math with a screen reader.
- **Teachers and companions**: the people who prepare materials, create exercises, and guide each student's learning.

## Features

- **Annoter** converts PDF modules into linear, screen-reader-friendly text. Teachers review and validate the result before students see it.
- **Quizzer** generates multiple-choice questions from a module, based on Bloom's Taxonomy. Every question, answer, and justification is editable.
- **Scriber** is an accessible digital workspace with a custom keyboard for entering math notation and recording each calculation step.
- **Evaluaizer** explains why an answer is right or wrong, down to the step where the reasoning went off track.
- **Clarifier** is a study assistant that answers from teacher-curated modules and cites its sources.
